----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Mora-et-al-2026_TLBM_geodynamical_modelling_of_solid_state_convection_and_magma_dynamics_and_strong_scaling_to_the_exascale.pdf

   TLBM theory, timescales and conversion between dimensional physical and dimensionless LBM properties,
   examples of solid state convection and turbulent magma ocean dynamics, performance and strong scaling
   to 300K cores for 3D mantle models at a 3km resolution, time projections for mantle modelling at a 3 km
   resolution and at up to Ra=10^17, Re and with Ra and Pr scaling with A for turbulent convection, 
   model size requirements to model at ultra-high Rayleigh numbers up to Ra=10^17, and sinking block test.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. Morra-et-al-2025_Benchmark_TLBM_cf_PDE_Navier_Stokes_solvers.pdf

   Benchmarks of the TLBM against two finite PR PDE solvers for Pr=1,10,100 and 1000,
   and comparison of Pr = 1000 benchmarks against an infinite Pr PDE solver. 
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. Mora-Yuen-2018_LBM_simulation_of_regimes_of_convection_and_plume_dynamics.pdf

   TLBM simulations of regimes of thermal convection at Ra from 1000 to 10^10 at Pr
   from 1 to 50K, and Nusselt number to Ra scaling for Ra = 10^4 through 10^7.5 at Pr=1.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. Mora-et-al-2020_Concise_Python_LBM_on_HPC_for_geo-fluid_flow.pdf

   Implementation of the LBM for HPC clusters in Python using mpi4py and weak scaling to 2K cores.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. Mora-et-al-2021_Optimal_surface_tension_RK_multiphase_LBM.pdf

   Optimal surface tension isotropy immiscible multiphase LBM.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. Mora-et-al-2023_Multiphase_LBM_three_phase_two_component_flow.pdf

   Multiphase LBM with two immiscible phases and a miscible component in one of the immiscible phases.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. Honarbakhsh-et-al-2026_(in_press)_Iron_silicate_segregation_in_convecting_magma_ocean_via_multiphase_TLBM.pdf

   Iron-silicate segregation within a convecting magma ocean via the multiphase TLBM and implications to iron-silicate mixing.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. Honarbakhsh-et-al-2026_(submitted)_Metal_silicate_segregation_during_planetary_accretion.pdf

   Immiscible multiphase TLBM of metal-silicate segregation during planetary accretion, limited
   iron emulsification through larger impacts, and higher mixing in deeper magma ocean layers.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. Bargees-et-al-2026_(in_preparation)_Immiscible_and_miscible_multiphase_TLBM_for_laminar_and_turbulent_flow.pdf

   Theory of multiphase TLBM capable of modelling both immiscible and miscible phases for solid state convection
   and magma ocean dynamics with examples including iron cloud breakup and decent through a turbulent magma ocean
   and partial melting of peridotite to TTG's and basalt.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. Mora-et-al-EGU-2026-TLBM-to-study-early-Earth.pptx

   EGU PICO presentation summarizing research progress to study early Earth with the TLBM.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. Morra-et-al-AGU2025-Magma_ocean_dynamics_with_PT_dependent_viscosity.pdf

   AGU poster on modelling P-T dependent viscosity to study turbulence in magma oceans and equilibriation in the Hadean.
